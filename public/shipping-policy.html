2. Shipping Charges & Free Thresholds

  • Orders of ₹499 and above: 100% Free Express Delivery across India.
  • Orders below ₹499: Standard subsidized flat shipping rate of ₹49 is applied at checkout to cover premium packaging.
  • No Hidden Costs: All prices displayed on our website are inclusive of all applicable GST and packing charges.

3. Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

  • Dispatch Window: Orders placed before 3:00 PM IST (Monday through Saturday) are typically dispatched on the same business day. Orders placed after 3:00 PM or on Sundays/Public Holidays are dispatched on the next working day.
  • Mumbai & Maharashtra: Delivered within 1 to 2 business days.
  • Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): Delivered within 2 to 4 business days.
  • Rest of India (Tier 2/3 cities and remote regions): Delivered within 3 to 5 business days.

4. Real-Time Tracking & Notifications

Once your package is handed over to our courier partner, you will receive real-time notifications containing your tracking link (AWB number) via SMS, WhatsApp, and email.

You can also track your live shipment anytime directly through our customer portal: Track Your Order in Customer Portal →

5. Luxury Tamper-Proof Packaging

Every solid perfume tin is enclosed in custom shock-absorbing foam inserts and placed inside a heavy-duty, tamper-evident security pouch. Our solid wax formulations are formulated to remain intact during standard transit temperatures, ensuring your fragrance reaches you in immaculate condition.
