1. Personal Care Hygiene Classification

Solid perfumes, balms, and personal fragrances are categorized as Personal Care & Hygiene Products. For health, safety, and sanitary standards:

  • Items that have been opened, sampled, swiped on skin, or had their protective seal breached cannot be returned for subjective preferences (such as personal scent preference).
  • We encourage customers exploring our collection for the first time to review the detailed olfactory pyramids (Top, Heart, and Base notes) displayed on each product page or reach out to our team for scent recommendations prior to purchase.

2. 48-Hour Transit Damage & Defect Claims

We provide a mandatory 48-hour replacement or refund window from the official timestamp of delivery if:

  • The package or inner aluminum tin arrived physically damaged, cracked, or crushed.
  • The solid wax leaked or melted significantly due to transit handling.
  • You received an incorrect scent or quantity mismatch against your confirmed order.

3. How to File a Claim (Simple 3-Step Process)

Step 1: Document Proof

Take a clear unboxing video or 2-3 photos clearly showing the damaged tin and shipping label.

Step 2: Message Us

Share your Order ID and media on WhatsApp at +91 86240 85212 or via our Contact Form.

Step 3: Instant Resolution

We will dispatch an express replacement in 24 hours or issue a full refund to your UPI/Bank account.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

When an approved refund is requested instead of a product replacement:

  • UPI / Prepaid Orders: Refund is initiated within 24 hours of claim verification directly to the customer's original UPI ID / bank account. Funds usually reflect within 24 to 48 banking hours.
  • Cash On Delivery (COD) Orders: In case of COD transit issues, the customer can provide their UPI VPA or NEFT bank details for direct transfer.

5. Order Cancellations

You may cancel your order at any time before the package has been dispatched from our studio (usually within 2-4 hours of order placement).

To cancel an order, simply message our team on WhatsApp at +91 86240 85212 with your Order ID. If already prepaid via UPI, a 100% full refund will be processed immediately without any cancellation fee. Once an order is handed over to the courier and an AWB is generated, cancellations cannot be made in transit.
