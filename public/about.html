Artisanal Fragrance House

The Art of Pure, Alcohol-Free Solid Perfumery

Born in Mumbai, ZAID'S PERFUMES was founded with a singular, uncompromising vision: to revolutionize everyday fragrance by eliminating toxic drying alcohols and replacing them with pure, skin-loving waxes and royal perfume extraits.

🌿

100% Organic Beeswax Base

Traditional commercial sprays evaporate in 90 minutes because they are 80-90% ethyl alcohol. Our solid perfumes melt gently at body temperature on your pulse points, locking the pure scent onto your skin for 8+ hours without synthetic fillers.

🛡️

Zero Leakage & Pocket Freedom

Fragile glass bottles break in gym bags and are confiscated at airport security. Our shatterproof aluminum pocket tins slip easily into your denim pocket, suit jacket, or carry-on bag for instant fragrance anytime, anywhere.

👑

High-Concentration Extraits

We formulate at Extrait de Parfum strength (over 30% pure fragrance oil concentration). Every blend is carefully hand-poured in small artisanal batches to guarantee peak sillage, projection, and notes clarity.

🇮🇳

Proudly Handcrafted in India

From our Bandra West fragrance studio in Mumbai to perfume connoisseurs across India, we take pride in authentic Indian craftsmanship, ethical sourcing, cruelty-free processes, and premium IFRA-compliant ingredients.
